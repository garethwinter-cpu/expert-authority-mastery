# Expert & Authority Mastery: Getting to Know You

**Onboarding survey · build spec for the team · 14 September 2026**

Built to the Mastery Onboarding Survey DNA (core block, then Goals & Motivation, then Experience & Skills; dropdowns by default; goals and skills mandatory; phone and income optional) and modelled on the live Speaking & Influence, Social Media and Spiritual Mastery forms. Every programme-specific question below exists because a week of the curriculum needs the answer.

## 3. Why these questions and not others

**Every programme-specific question maps to a week.** B1 to B5 set the stage for Weeks 1 to 4 (the visibility wound, the identity shift, the edge, the positioning). C1 to C4 are the Week 14 ladder inventory taken in advance. C5 sizes the AI scaffolding for Weeks 7, 9 and 10. D3 gives each guest teacher the room's fears for their week. D4 is the Authority Blueprint as a checklist. E1 and E2 are the two before-and-after numbers the programme can report on at graduation.

**Five questions mirror the summit quiz word for word** (B2, B3, B5, D1, and the four-type framing). The quiz has 626 registrants and rising. Keeping the wording identical means we can compare who registered with who bought, and we can join a student's quiz answers to their survey answers without a translation table.

**Open text is used four times only**, each where a dropdown is genuinely impossible: the one-sentence expertise (B1), audience size (C4), desired transformation (D2) and the supplementary field (F4). Everything else is closed for clean analysis.

**Two things are deliberately not asked.** We do not ask which guest teachers they want to hear from, because the faculty is set. We do not ask about desired income, because that field belongs to Manifesting Mastery only under the DNA.
