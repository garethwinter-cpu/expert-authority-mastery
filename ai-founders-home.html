AI for Founders Mastery

AI Mastery goes deep into AI. AI for Founders goes deep into the business. Sixteen weeks in which a founder rebuilds the business one function at a time, strategy to finance, each with a ready-made system installed and running. AI is the means, not the subject. You leave as the orchestrator of a company that does more with a leaner team, with your Founder Operating System installed and running: the company brain, the marketing and sales engines, the delivery and operations agents, and the founder’s cockpit.

The curriculum

Eight modules, one business pillar each

Who it is for

Two founders in the room

From the AI for Founders quiz and the AI Mastery buyer surveys. Both have something real to sell. Both have growth capped by their own hours. They feel it differently, and the two tracks in every lab are built on that difference.

The solo founder

"Your clients book you, not your business."

Coaches, consultants, practitioners and creators who are the business. Six in ten quiz takers. Content and social media eat a quarter of their week, then marketing, then leads and follow-up. They start with marketing, and the voice work in Module 3 is where the week changes first.

The founder with a team of up to ten

"Decisions and complaints always go through me, and that wears me down."

Consulting and professional services lead. One in five quiz takers, and they buy at nearly twice the solo rate. Email, admin and approvals eat their week. They start with operations, and the company brain in Module 2 is the first thing the team stops asking them for.

      The boundary

      Neither is the advanced one

      AI Mastery makes you a builder. AI for Founders makes you an orchestrator. Either order works: Mastery first and you extend the Founders systems yourself; Founders first and Mastery teaches you to build what is not in the packs. Never "Mastery 2", and never the beginner product. The line against Expert & Authority, the other Mastery in this pathway, is on the positioning page: that one fixes demand, this one fixes capacity.
