What the community answers

Why it is built this way

They are warm. They came to the summit, took the quiz or sat in Expert & Authority. The survey treats them as partners: it asks about their business before it asks about ours, asks for stories rather than opinions, shows them the outline inside the form, and ends by asking how far they want to go. People value what they help build, so the last section is the point, not an afterthought. Under every question is the line marketing will want: why we ask, and what it decides in the curriculum.
