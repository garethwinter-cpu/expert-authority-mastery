# Benchmark: real Mastery lesson titles

Lesson titles from past Mindvalley Masteries, read from each programme's Airtable base in October 2026, with how each session did.

- **Pull** is the live audience for that session divided by the median of the four nearest sessions in the same cohort. It controls for the normal drop-off across a programme, so 1.30 means about 30% more people came live than to the sessions around it. Above 1.2 is strong, below 0.8 is weak.
- **Rating** is the average post-session rating out of 10, with the number of ratings. It measures the session, not the title, so use it to spot strong teachers and topics, not strong titles.
- Teacher, topic, timing and promotion also move these numbers. Read the patterns across many titles, never one row.

## What the numbers say

Entrepreneurship Mastery 2025, 68 sessions with a pull score:

| Title pattern | Sessions | Median pull |
|---|---|---|
| Has "AI" in the title | 6 | 0.57 |
| No "AI" in the title | 62 | 1.25 |
| Topic, then a promise ("Topic: promise" or "Topic — promise") | 18 | 1.36 |
| No topic-and-promise split | 50 | 0.93 |

Directional, small samples, but they point one way: an audience that already bought an AI or business programme is pulled by the business outcome, not by the word "AI", and a plain topic followed by a promise tells them at a glance what the session is and why it matters.

Other patterns worth knowing:

- **Plain business topics pull.** "Negotiation", "Mastering PR", "Writing Copy that Sells", "Designing Websites that Convert" all drew 1.4 to 1.6 times their neighbours.
- **"How to do X with AI" pulls least.** "Writing Newsletters with AI" (0.44), "What to Build with AI (and What to Avoid)" (0.46), "Writing Social Content with AI" (0.55).
- **Generic labels fail.** "Community Session" and "Community Call #4" are among the weakest sessions in their cohorts.
- **A named framework needs a promise beside it.** "The CPR Framework: Scaling Without Chaos" pulled 1.85; the name alone would mean nothing to a student who has not met it.
- **The top-rated sessions have outcome titles.** "Find, Understand, and Win Your Best Customers" (9.68), "Generate Any Image or Graphic Asset" (9.62), "Build the App You Always Wished Existed" (9.28).
