## The calls behind them

- **Lessons got a plain topic first.** Website, Offer and Pricing, Copywriting, Search, Inbox, Branding, Funnels, Content, Meta Ads, Bookkeeping, Lead Scoring, Sales Calls, Dashboard, Handover. A student scanning the list knows each session's subject in two seconds.
- **Good promises were kept, not replaced.** "Who Buys, What to Sell, What to Charge", "Close More, Chase Less", "Never Chase a Bad Lead" and "Run Your Company From One Screen" were already strong; they only gained a topic.
- **Jargon left the titles.** "Offer book", "answer hub", "owner pages", "Monday brief" and "clinic" are words the team uses, not words a student knows before the session.
- **One named concept stayed, explained.** "Company Brain: Never Explain Your Business Twice" uses the programme's own term in the topic slot and the promise says what it means.
- **Labs became instructions with a result.** Each starts with a build verb and names the thing the student leaves with: "Launch Your Website and Test Your Lead Form".
- **The quiz's own words were reused.** "What to Automate First" was the most repeated question in the AI for Founders quiz (23 times word for word), so the bonus call keeps it.
- **Series names stayed.** "Get AI-Ready 1 and 2" is the onboarding brand; only the promise after the colon changed.
- **Teacher names came out.** "Built With Vishen" moved to the teacher field, so the title survives a line-up change.
