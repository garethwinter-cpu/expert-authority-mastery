# Approved examples

Match the rhythm of these: one paragraph, a heading, a short list. Examples come from Expert & Authority Mastery 2026 and AI for Founders Mastery 2026. The Expert & Authority ones are lightly edited to today's rules, with no dashes and the standard headings.

## Lesson

**Why Most Experts Stay Invisible** (Expert & Authority, week 1)

The gap between expertise and authority isn't talent. It's positioning. This week Vishen breaks down why brilliant people stay unknown, what separates the experts who get paid from those who get ignored, and how to identify the category you were born to own. You'll map your current authority level, pinpoint your unfair advantage, and write the one-sentence statement that makes you the only logical choice in your space.

You'll learn:
- The Authority Gap: why credentials don't equal influence
- The 3 stages of expert evolution, and where you are now
- How to own a category instead of just occupying one
- How to write your one-sentence authority statement

**Design a Funnel That Turns Visitors Into Leads** (AI for Founders, week 7)

A website brings visitors. A funnel turns them into leads. This week Vishen shows you how to choose the funnel that fits your business: a booked call if you sell services, a purchase if you sell products, a trial if you sell software. You'll design your lead magnet, the free checklist, guide or scorecard you promised on your site, and the nurture emails that follow it, all written in your company's voice. This week you design it; next week you launch it.

You'll learn:
- Which of three funnel types fits your business
- How to create a lead magnet people actually want
- How to write nurture emails that lead to your offer
- How to map your funnel before you build it

## Workshop or lab

**Persuasion Vision workshop** (Expert & Authority). This is the example the team pointed to as the standard.

Vishen gave you the persuasion principles in the lesson. In this session, Vykintas has you rewrite your actual core message against them, line by line, until Cialdini's 7 principles and the 5 emotional triggers are built into your own words instead of someone else's example. You'll read the rewritten version aloud to the room, standing in for your ideal buyer, and adjust it in real time based on what lands.

You'll leave with:
- Your core message rewritten using the 7 principles and 5 triggers
- That message read aloud and tested on your ideal buyer

**Your Site, Live by Thursday** (AI for Founders, week 1 lab)

The lesson showed you a website built live. In this session, Vykintas has you build and launch yours. If your hosting isn't set up yet, you'll do it together first. Then you'll refine your site with your own photos and reviews, publish it with one sentence, connect a custom domain if you want one, and test your lead form with a real enquiry. You'll also start your 30-minute hours log, the number your graduation is measured against. Before the session ends, your link goes in the community thread.

You'll leave with:
- Your website live, built by you and your AI team
- Your lead form tested end to end
- Your hours log started

## Graduation

**Graduation and Demo Day** (AI for Founders)

This is where we close the program together. Your hours log from before week 1 is counted again: how many hours your business needs from you now, against where you started. Then it's Demo Day, as founders from the cohort each show one system, live. You'll leave with:

## Before and after

**Copy That Turns Visitors Into Buyers** (AI for Founders, week 3).

### Before
This was internal notes in the Description field, which is what the team flagged:

The Head of Copywriting runs the workflow: the conversion auditor scores the week 1 site, the conversion copywriter rewrites it from the offer book, the case study writer turns your best customer story into proof, and the auditor scores it again. The before and after number is the lesson's proof.

Then the voice file: how the company sounds, the words it uses and never uses, written from your best pages and your own speech. It is short, it is the company's voice, not a personal brand, and it goes into the company brain next week. The inbox team reads it there in week 5, the funnel in weeks 7 and 8, the content team in week 10. The lead magnet is promised on the page this week and built in week 7.

The line with Expert & Authority: that programme finds a person's distinctive voice. This lesson writes conversion copy for a company and keeps it consistent.

### After
The teacher wasn't confirmed, so the lesson is written without a name:

A good-looking website that doesn't convert is just a brochure. This week you'll put your site through a proper conversion audit: score it, rewrite it from your offer book, turn your best customer story into proof, and score it again, so you can see exactly how much better it got. Then you'll write your company's voice file: how your business sounds, the words it uses and the words it never uses. From next week, every AI team you build writes in that voice.

You'll learn:
- How to audit and score a page for conversion
- How to rewrite your copy around what your buyers actually want
- How to turn one customer story into a case study that sells
- How to capture your company's voice so all your AI writing sounds like you
