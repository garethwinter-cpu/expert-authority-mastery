The architecture

Six pillars: the same guild shape, tuned for founders

1

The Floor

Application only, concierge review, monthly intake, kick-and-refund for takers: the constitution Vishen stated from stage. The qualifying bar for this guild is an open decision: our proposal is a live business with a Founder OS in production (Mastery certification or equivalent), plus a revenue floor to be set with the Academy. The E&A Guild's $100K precedent suggests the shape. Adopted from the Academy playbook: the Mastery cohort's top 10 by contribution are auto-invited at graduation, announced live — the invitation waives the application, never the bar or the fee.

2

The Pulse

The monthly State of the Stack: model releases that change the maths, tool consolidations, cost-curve shifts, what broke and what got cheap. Plus live teardowns: a member's real pipeline, benchmarked in the open. The Mastery teaches doctrine; the Guild reports from the front, monthly, because the front moves monthly.

3

The Commons

The shared-systems library: working agents, skills, automations and prompts posted by members, with giver leaderboards. When one founder cracks delivery automation, every forge in the guild gets the technique. The blacksmith rule, applied to agents. Two years of members' running systems becomes a moat no course can copy.

4

The Upgrade Sprint

The pillar unique to this guild: quarterly OS upgrade sprints: a build week where every member's Founder Operating System is re-benchmarked against the newest tools, with lab coaches on the floor. This absorbs the Mastery's "Alumni & OS Upgrades" continuity rung and gives the $10K its most concrete deliverable: your machine, serviced four times a year.

5

Pods & Witnessing

Always-on founder pods of 6–12: numbers named aloud, the weekly review, the Friday text. For founders the witnessing unit doubles as a board: the pod sees your dashboard, not your highlight reel.

6

The Moot

Real-world founder tables in member cities, quarterly Guild Launch windows where the room gets behind whoever is shipping (intros, first customers, the collective push), and MVU as the annual moot, where the year's best systems are demoed from stage. Entry gets an initiation, not a receipt: identity is environmental.

Commercial logic

Same tier, same anchor, different deliverable

The anchor

$10,000 a year

The guild tier Vishen defined from stage ("$10,000 masterminds… may go up to 20,000 a year"), applied to this pathway. Annual-first with a quarterly payment plan; founding members lock the founding price. The AI-tools processing add-on (~$49.99/month) is already priced from stage.

The model

Evergreen, capacity-capped

Monthly application intake; capacity caps per pod. The Certify tier of the Mastery ($7,999, Certified AI Consultant) feeds the guild its most valuable members: certified installers whose client work generates the commons' best material.

The economics

Recurring, self-generating content

Illustrative, not a target: 200 members is $2M ARR, recurring. The content cost falls over time, because the members' systems, benchmarks and teardowns are the programme. The Guild is also the churn answer for the whole pathway — renewal is the only metric that matters, per the Academy playbook — and the reason "graduating" from the Mastery doesn't mean leaving Mindvalley.

What this is not

Held boundaries

  • Not a course, and not the Mastery again. No five-pillars-from-scratch. A member's first question is never "how do I build a company brain". It's "what should I rebuild this quarter."
  • Not AI Mastery's upgrade path. AI Mastery transforms you; its graduates' next step is the AIF Mastery if their business is the bottleneck. This Guild sits above the Founders line only.
  • Not Zoom-only. Founder tables, upgrade sprints and the moot are the product; calls are connective tissue.
  • Not a promise of funding. Guild Launches mean the room moves for you: intros, first customers, the collective push. Anything resembling co-investment needs a legal read before it is written anywhere.
The funnel, complete

AI First Founder Quest → AI for Founders Mastery (install it, $5,999/$7,999) → systems in production → Guild (keep it winning, $10,000/yr). The Mastery's final systems audit hands each graduate a maintenance plan, and the maintenance plan's natural home is the Guild. The Academy's one-pager already routes it this way; this page makes the routing official.

Decisions

Open before this goes external

Open
  • The floor. The E&A Guild inherits Vishen's stated $100K bar; this guild's equivalent (OS in production plus a revenue floor) needs setting with the Academy before applications open.
  • The name. The Academy's note calls it "the AI Guild"; this proposal says AI for Founders Guild so the tier reads as the Mastery's roof, not a separate brand. Sign off with Vishen + Jaideep.
  • Reconcile with the Academy's Guild #1 pilot (~50 members, admissions this quarter, no stated price or floor in its playbook) against this proposal's stage-validated $10,000/yr tier. One spec, one owner.
  • One commons or two. We recommend separate rooms for the two guilds (the pulse content diverges completely) on shared Finerminds infrastructure. Confirm the platform carries leaderboards, pods and the systems library.
  • Guildmaster. A permanent operator-host; Vykintas is the obvious candidate given the lab data, but that allocation trades against the Mastery. Decide once, across both.
Decided in this proposal
  • $10,000/yr anchor, annual-first: the stage-defined guild tier, with the $20K escalation as founding urgency
  • Evergreen with monthly intake; six-pillar architecture; quarterly OS upgrade sprints as the signature deliverable
  • The Guild never re-teaches the Mastery; without an OS in production, the route is the Mastery first
  • Absorbs the "Alumni & OS Upgrades" continuity rung. One product, not two
