# AI for Founders: the time slot, with the numbers · 7 October 2026

**Question (Gareth, 7 Oct):** do past Masteries give us data to back the 9am Pacific slot, or does Janja's 3pm London
look better? **Short answer: keep 9am Pacific.** Nothing in five Masteries' attendance records shows the hour, inside the
7am to 10am Pacific window, moving live turnout. What moves it is the week of the programme (weeks 1 and 2 draw two to
three times the late-programme room) and the day (Friday is the weak day wherever it was tested). Our audience is
roughly 60 percent North American in every Mastery, so once the hour is shown not to cost attendance, the slot that
suits the majority wins. 9am Pacific is also the exact slot the last two AI Mastery cohorts ran at, with the same
kind of audience.

Source: the Mastery Airtable bases (AI Summit & Mastery, Manifesting, Social Media, Speaking & Influence,
Entrepreneurship), Lessons and Schedule tables, fields "Attendees" (unique Zoom attendees per session), "Rec. Views
(1 week)", "Enrolled", and the onboarding-survey "Location" links. Pulled 7 Oct 2026. Bonus calls, kick-offs and
graduations excluded from the medians. Times are read from the Pacific datetime in Airtable and converted, so London
hours already reflect clock changes.

## 2. The one natural experiment: Entrepreneurship Mastery moved slot mid-cohort

The same students, the same two days, the slot moved from 5pm London (9am PT) to 3pm London (7am PT) over the
Christmas break. If the hour mattered for a mostly North American room, January would show a step.

| Period | Slot | Attendance per session |
|---|---|---|
| 9 to 18 Dec 2025 (last four at the old slot) | 5pm London / 9am PT | 262, 235, 238, 228 |
| 6 to 22 Jan 2026 (first five at the new slot) | 3pm London / 7am PT | 165, 216, 213, 232, 216 |
| Feb to Apr 2026 | 3pm then 2pm London / 7am PT | 146 to 203, settling near 175 |

No step in either direction. The January numbers continue the ordinary decline every Mastery shows (see section 3)
and then flatten. Moving to 3pm London bought nothing, and cost nothing, for attendance.

The one session that ran on Thanksgiving Day itself kept about 70 percent of its normal room, and its week-1 recording

day after showed no meaningful dip. This sharpens the 5 October reasoning, which rested on Expert & Authority's
week-level reading: the week is fine, the day costs roughly a quarter to a third of the live room, and the recording
recovers it. Our Week 2 lab falls on Thursday 26 November 2026, Thanksgiving Day. It stays, as a Vykintas lab,
recorded, with the Week 3 lesson on Tuesday 1 December picking up anyone who missed it. The data-favoured alternative,
if the team wants it, is Wednesday 25 November: the day before showed no dip, and it costs one break in the
Tuesday/Thursday rhythm.

## 4. Who is in the room

Onboarding-survey locations, students who gave one:

| Base | Students | USA | Canada | UK | North America | Europe | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI Mastery 2025 + 2026 | 515 | 48% | 8% | 7% | 59% | 25% | 16% |
| Manifesting Mastery | 782 | 51% | 8% | 7% | 61% | 26% | 13% |

Speaking & Influence and Social Media survey samples (around 150 to 270 each) show the same shape: the United States
about half, the United Kingdom under a tenth. The UK is the fourth or fifth market in every Mastery, after the US,
Canada and roughly level with Germany, Switzerland and the Netherlands combined.

What the two candidate slots look like for those people:

| Slot | US West | US East | UK | Central Europe | Dubai | Sydney |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9am Pacific (ours) | 9am | noon | 5pm | 6pm | 9pm | 4am |
| 7am Pacific (3pm London) | 7am | 10am | 3pm | 4pm | 7pm | 2am |

9am Pacific keeps the whole North American majority inside working hours and keeps Europe before dinner. 3pm London
improves the UK and Dubai evening and puts the West Coast, our single largest region, at 7am. The attendance data
says that trade is attendance-neutral, so make it on comfort for the majority: 9am Pacific.

## 5. Recordings do not recover a missed live session

Week-1 recording views run at about a quarter of the live count in AI Mastery (23 to 26 percent), Social Media (24 to
26 percent) and Entrepreneurship (23 percent), a third in Speaking & Influence (30 percent). Manifesting is the outlier
at 53 percent. For a programme whose promise is "run on AI" and whose labs are hands-on, the live room is the product;
that argues for the slot the majority can make, not the slot that is kindest to a minority who will watch back anyway.

## 6. Precedent on clock changes

The AI Mastery 2025 and 2026 cohorts and Social Media 2026 all held Pacific time constant and let London move (their
London hour reads 4pm in some weeks and 5pm in others). That is the convention. For AI for Founders, 9am Pacific is
5pm London for 16 of 17 weeks and 4pm London for the final week (from 14 March 2027, when US clocks move first). Tell
students the Pacific time; show London as the derived time.

## 7. Recommendation and what to tell Janja

1. **Keep 9am Pacific, Tuesday and Thursday.** The last two AI Mastery cohorts ran there with 29 to 38 percent median
   live attendance, in line with or above the other Masteries.
2. **The hour does not move attendance in our records.** Entrepreneurship Mastery moved from 5pm to 3pm London
   mid-cohort and saw no change beyond the normal fade. Speaking & Influence's high rates at 3pm London come with
   cohorts under 500, not with the hour.
3. **Day and week matter more.** No Fridays; Vishen in weeks 1 and 2, where the room is two to three times the size.
4. **Audience is about 60 percent North American and about 7 percent UK**, in every Mastery we have surveyed.

For Janja: "Thanks for raising 3pm London. We looked at attendance across AI, Manifesting, Social Media, Speaking and
Entrepreneurship Mastery. When Entrepreneurship Mastery moved from 5pm to 3pm London in January the attendance line
did not move, and roughly 60 percent of every Mastery's students are in North America against about 7 percent in the
UK. So we are holding 9am Pacific, which is 5pm London for all but the final week, and we will publish both times."

## Caveats

- "Attendees" is the team's per-session unique Zoom count; "Rec. Views (1 week)" is the team's count a week after
  each call. Both are as recorded, not re-derived.
- The Entrepreneurship switch is the only within-cohort comparison; it is confounded with the usual mid-programme
  fade, which is why the reading is "no step", not "no effect".
- AI Mastery 2024 and Manifesting 2026 have no enrolment figure in the base, so their live rates are not shown.
- The location data comes from onboarding surveys, which a third to a half of students complete; the shape is
  consistent across five bases but it is a sample.
