## 1. The recommendation in one paragraph

Treat one thing, enough-ness, at the two moments this room flinches: being seen and being paid. Week 1 made every student publish. Week 2 is the morning after, when the mind says "who do you think you are". Marisa treats the visibility wound at the root (UL Lesson 14), runs her hypnotic exercise live (UL Lesson 15), arms the room against the first critical comment (UL Lesson 16, RTHA Lesson 9), and plants the number the room will need in Week 14 by running her Financial Upper Limit exercise once (RTHA Lesson 25). She leaves the room on her own 21-day protocol, and the 21st day lands on Tuesday 4 November, the day Module 2 opens.

## 2. What the room is arriving with

- 7,739 quiz responses: 69% identify as "The Expert · deep expertise but nobody knows I exist"; 48% chose "nobody knows I exist" as the breakdown; 50% have never sold anything.
- 428 people wrote some version of "I'm good at what I do, but…". That sentence is the wound.
- Only 6% named fear as their problem. They call it "I need a system". Marisa's job is to show them the system is downstream of the belief.
- Week 1 outcome: every student has published one awkward video, live, in-session, and handled the first comment with the group watching. Week 2 opens with that still fresh.
- Two survey baselines already captured: comfort on camera (1 to 10) and comfort asking to be paid (1 to 10). Both are re-asked at graduation. This session is the intervention on the first, and seeds the second.

## 3. The two applications we are asking her to make (ours, not hers)

1. **"Being seen" as the wound.** Her material treats enough-ness generally. We ask her to point it at visibility: the mind that said at age six "I don't want people to look at me" (RTHA Lesson 5, "your mind does what it thinks you want") is the same mind that will not let an expert post, go live, or say "I help…".
2. **"Being paid" as the second wound.** One segment only, ten minutes, using her own Financial Upper Limit exercise (RTHA Lesson 25) and her line "you cannot be rejected unless you allow people to reject you… I can ask for that pay rise, I can ask for funding" (RTHA Lesson 8). The number each student writes goes into their Authority Blueprint tracker and is opened again at the Week 14 Pricing Clinic with Daniel Priestley.

Everything else in the run of show is her lesson, in her order of ideas.
